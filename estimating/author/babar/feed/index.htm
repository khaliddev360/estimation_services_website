How are Building Estimates calculated in The US?

There are a few ways to estimate the cost of building a structure in the United States. Construction estimating services providers and construction estimators in USA also work on these methods to get accurate estimating results for you.  This article will explore three of the most common methods: square footage, unit price, and construction cost index. 

Square Footage Method:

The square footage method is one of the simplest ways to estimate building costs. It involves calculating the total area of the structure and multiplying it by a predetermined rate per square foot. This rate can vary depending on the location and type of system being built.

For example, the area of your construction site is 1500 sq ft, while the construction cost is 1500 per square ft

1500 * 1500 = 2250000

Your total project cost will be 2250000. 

Unit Price Method:

The unit price method is another popular way to estimate building costs. It involves calculating the cost of each component or “unit” that goes into constructing the structure. This includes lumber, nails, screws, drywall, etc. Once you have calculated the cost of all units, you simply add them up to get an overall price for construction. To make it understandable, here is a sample:

  • Material Cost: $165,000  
  • Framing Cost:$45,0000
  • Exterior Work Cost $35,000
  • Systems Installation Cost: $32,500
  • Interior Finishes: $52,000

To make the total sum of your home building cost as per unit price method will be estimated by adding all the above-mentioned estimations. However, your total cost is subject to inflation or other groundwork realities. 

Construction Cost Index Method: 

The construction cost index (CCI) method is a more sophisticated way to estimate building costs. It takes into account changes in material prices and labor rates over time. By using historical data from past projects, builders can create an accurate estimate of how much a new project will cost.

Construction takeoff is the first step in creating an accurate estimate, and it requires a complete set of construction drawings printed to scale, some highlighters, measuring tools (such as a digital scale master), and a way to capture and save the quantities as you perform the quantity takeoff and then transfer them to your estimating solution.
